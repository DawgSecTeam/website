Who we are

We're a group of students passionate about competitive cyber, and together we make up the premier cyber defense team in Maryland, and we've skillmogged just about every other team that's dared to challenge us.

How we operate

We have two meetings a week - a Technical Workshop from 7:15-8:15 PM on Tuesdays, covering topics like baselining, SQL databases, and Windows GPOs. Team meetings are on Thursdays from 6:15-8:15 PM. Both are in the Performing Arts Building (PAHB), Room 108.

Can I get involved?

Indubiously! We'd love to see you. Come in person to a Technical Workshop to learn something new or one of the Thursday Team Meetings to get help with a challenge or learn more about cyber competitions. At the end of each semester, you can try out to get a spot on our competition team.

What we build

Towards the purpose of increasing our skill in cyber competitions, we've built numerous tools of varying complexity, with some "automated assistance". Scoring engines, vulnerability databases, and automated environments for spinning up practice competitions.

Python

huitzilopochtli

A CyPat/eCitadel-style scoring engine for individual and ranked practice.

Python

nakon

A vulnerability and misconfig deployment tool for our internal practice competitions.

Terraform

tezcatlipoca

The tool we use to spin up practice competitions, built with Terraform and using Nakon.
